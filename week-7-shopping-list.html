Week 7 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • Potatoes, 3kg bag (covers gratin, pork bowls, tenders and korma chips)
  • 5 onions (plus 1 red onion for the korma)
  • 1 small leek (optional swap for onion on the gratin)
  • 4 peppers
  • 1 little gem lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • Spinach, 1 bag / handful
  • 1 garlic clove (fresh, for the gratin)

Meat & Fish

  • 2 boxes frozen fish fingers
  • Chicken thigh fillets, 1.6kg total (orzo 500g, tenders 500g, korma 600g)
  • 500g beef mince
  • 400g pork sausage meat
  • 400g pork steaks

Fridge / Dairy

  • Double cream, 300ml
  • Whole milk, 150ml (or a small bottle)
  • Crème fraîche, 300ml pot (covers orzo, pork bowls and korma)
  • Cheddar, 350–400g
  • Butter, 1 tbsp (or use from the pack)
  • 2 eggs

Bakery

  • Soft flour tortillas, 6–8

Basics / Packets

Freeze leftover passata in an ice-cube tray if you do not use the whole carton.

  • Orzo, 300g
  • Pasta, 350g
  • Passata, 500g carton
  • Tomato purée
  • Chicken stock (400ml made-up, or stock cubes)
  • Plain flour, 50g
  • Dried breadcrumbs, 100g

Store Cupboard

Only buy what you have run out of — most families already have these.

  • Cooking oil + oil spray
  • Salt & pepper
  • Garlic granules
  • Paprika + smoked paprika
  • Ground cumin
  • Dried oregano
  • Korma paste
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise

Shopping list optimised: Potatoes, chicken thighs, cheddar, crème fraîche, peppers, onions and garlic granules work across multiple teas to cut waste.
