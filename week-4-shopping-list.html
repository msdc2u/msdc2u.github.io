Week 4 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • 3.5kg potatoes
  • 3 leeks
  • 4 onions
  • 5 peppers
  • 500g carrots
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 2 little gem lettuces
  • 6 tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 bunch spring onions

Meat & Fish

  • 16 boneless skinless chicken thigh fillets (approx 1.2kg)
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 4 pork steaks
  • 500g beef mince
  • 4 rashers back bacon
  • 4 pollock fillets (approx 500–600g)
  • 2 x 160g tins tuna in spring water

Dairy

  • 300g cheddar cheese
  • 150g natural yoghurt (optional)
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml double cream

Bakery / Basics

  • 350g pasta (penne or rigatoni)
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 4 flatbreads or wraps

Store Cupboard

  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • Tomato purée
  • 2 fish or chicken stock cubes
  • 2 beef stock cubes
  • Cooking oil
  • Dried chives
  • Smoked paprika
  • Ground cumin
  • Chilli flakes (optional)
  • Garlic granules
  • Dried mixed herbs
  • Dried oregano
  • Dried parsley
  • Honey
  • Wholegrain mustard
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Lemon juice or malt vinegar
  • Salt & black pepper

Shopping List Optimised: Chicken thighs, potatoes, peppers and onions are used across multiple meals to help reduce waste. Every item earns its place — no protein is used more than twice, carbs are balanced (1 pasta dish, 1 rice dish, the rest potato-based), and flatbreads are used only once.
