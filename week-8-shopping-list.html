Week 8 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • 2 red peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 2 red onions
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 800g potatoes

Meat & Fish

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1.2kg boneless skinless chicken thigh fillets
  • 225g cooking chorizo
  • 400g beef mince
  • 4 pork steaks
  • 8 pork chipolatas or thin sausages
  • 8 rashers streaky bacon

Fridge / Dairy

  • 300ml crème fraîche
  • 200g grated cheddar
  • 125g mozzarella
  • 1 pint milk
  • Chicken stock cubes (enough for about 700ml / 3 cubes)

Basics / Packets

  • 650g pasta
  • 250g long-grain rice
  • 1 pack ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 4–8 flatbreads
  • 100g frozen peas
  • Sweet chilli sauce
  • Sweet onion marmalade
  • Peanut butter
  • Salted peanuts (small bag)

Store Cupboard

Only buy what you have run out of — most families already have these.

  • Cooking oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Garlic granules
  • Onion granules
  • Dried chives
  • Dried mixed herbs
  • Dried oregano
  • Smoked paprika
  • Chinese five-spice
  • Chilli flakes
  • Ground ginger
  • Cornflour
  • Tomato purée
  • Soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Honey
  • Maple syrup

Shopping list optimised: Crème fraîche, spring onions, cheese, sweet chilli, honey, soy, chicken thighs and stock cubes work across multiple teas to cut waste.
