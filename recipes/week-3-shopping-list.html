Week 3 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • 2 red peppers
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 onions
  • 1.5kg potatoes (for chips + chunks + mash + sauté)
  • 4 large baking potatoes
  • 300g broccoli florets
  • 200g frozen sweetcorn

Meat & Fish

  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 chicken thighs (bone-in)
  • 8 chicken thighs (boneless)
  • 500g beef mince
  • 500g pork mince
  • 200g bacon lardons
  • 400g white fish fillets (cod or haddock)

Dairy

  • 150g cheddar cheese
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 50g cheddar cheese (extra)
  • 150ml double cream
  • 1 egg

Bakery / Basics

  • 700g pasta (400g + 300g)
  • 300g uncooked rice
  • 2 × 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 100g breadcrumbs

Store Cupboard

  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tsp garlic granules
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried mixed herbs / parsley
  • 6 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt & black pepper

Shopping List Optimised: Chicken, cheese, potatoes, onions and frozen veg are used across multiple meals to help reduce waste.
