'Marry Me' Chicken Thighs with Cheesy Mini Roast Sauté Potatoes

Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 30 mins
Serves 4
Low cost

Ingredients

Serves 4 people
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 200ml single cream
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 50g grated Parmesan or hard cheese
  • 600g potatoes, cut into small roasties
  • 50g grated cheese, for the potatoes
  • Garlic granules, salt & pepper

Method

1

Heat oven to 200°C. Toss the cut potatoes in oil and seasoning, and roast for 30–35 minutes until golden.

2

Season the chicken thighs with paprika, garlic granules and pepper. Fry in a little oil for 5–6 minutes each side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.

3

In the same pan, add the cream, tomato purée and mixed herbs. Simmer for 3–4 minutes.

4

Stir in the Parmesan until melted into a smooth sauce. Return the chicken to the pan and coat well.

5

Scatter grated cheese over the roast potatoes for the last 5 minutes of cooking.

6

Serve the creamy chicken thighs with the cheesy roast potatoes.

Tips & Notes

  • A creamy, crowd-pleasing sauce that turns simple chicken thighs into something special, with minimal extra effort.
  • The potatoes are just cut small for quicker roasting — no need for baby potatoes.
  • Leftover sauce is great spooned over rice the next day.
