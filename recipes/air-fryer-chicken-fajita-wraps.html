Air Fryer Chicken Fajita Wraps

Tender chicken strips with peppers and onions, cooked in the air fryer and served in wraps. A quick and tasty fakeaway.

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 600g boneless chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 peppers, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tsp garlic granules
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 4 tortilla wraps
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Place the sliced chicken, peppers and onion in a bowl. Add the oil, garlic granules, paprika, chilli flakes, salt and pepper. Mix well.
  2. Tip everything into the air fryer basket. Cook at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway, until the chicken is cooked through and slightly charred.
  3. Warm the tortilla wraps.
  4. Spoon the chicken fajita mix into the wraps and serve.
Tip: Serve with grated cheese, lettuce or a spoonful of sour cream if you have it.
