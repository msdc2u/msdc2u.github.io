Chunky Sausage & Tomato Pasta Bake

Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 35 mins
Serves 4
Low cost

Ingredients

Serves 4 people
  • 8 pork sausages, cut into 3–4 chunks each
  • 350g pasta (penne or rigatoni)
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 peppers, chopped
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 tsp garlic granules
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 150g cheddar or mozzarella, grated
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & pepper

Method

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

2

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan. Fry the sausage chunks until browned on all sides. Remove to a plate.

3

In the same pan fry the onion and peppers for 5 minutes. Add the garlic granules and oregano, cook for 1 minute.

4

Stir in the tomato purée and both tins of tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes. Season.

5

Meanwhile cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet instructions. Drain.

6

Mix the pasta and sauce together, tip into a large ovenproof dish. Tuck in the sausage chunks, scatter over the cheese and bake for 20 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Tips & Notes

  • Make-ahead friendly — assemble the night before and bake when needed.
  • Uses cheap sausages and reheats well for lunch the next day.
  • Swap mozzarella for extra cheddar if you prefer.
