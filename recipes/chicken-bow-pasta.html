Chicken Bow Pasta

Chicken, pasta and a simple creamy sauce with a handful of spinach stirred through at the end.

Prep: 10 mins
Cook: 20 mins
Serves 4
Low cost

Ingredients

Serves4 people
  • 350g farfalle or any pasta
  • 500g boneless skinless chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 300ml crème fraîche
  • 80g grated Parmigiano Reggiano
  • 1 tsp mixed dried herbs
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 100g spinach
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

1

Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until just tender, then drain.

2

Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Season the chicken with paprika, salt and pepper and cook until golden and cooked through.

3

Add the onion and garlic granules. Cook until the onion has softened.

4

Stir in the crème fraîche, Parmigiano Reggiano and mixed herbs until smooth.

5

Add the pasta and spinach. Toss until the spinach wilts and everything is coated. Simmer for 2-3 minutes.

6

Serve warm.
