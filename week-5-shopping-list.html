Week 5 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • Potatoes — 1.2kg needed (roast potatoes + wedges) — buy a standard 2.5kg bag
  • 2 sweet potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 red peppers
  • 1 tomato
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Spring onions

Meat & Fish

  • 8 pork sausages
  • Chicken thigh fillets — for the wraps: 2 per adult, 1–1½ per child (2 wraps per adult, 1 thigh per wrap); plus 8 thighs for 'Marry Me' chicken. Work out your wrap total from your own family numbers, then add 8.
  • 100g chorizo
  • 500g beef mince
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 4 gammon steaks

Dairy

  • Single cream, 300ml (chorizo pasta 100ml + 'Marry Me' chicken 200ml)
  • Grated cheese, approx 250–300g bag (chorizo pasta, 'Marry Me' potatoes, meatball bake)
  • Parmesan or hard cheese, 50g
  • 1 egg
  • Mayonnaise (wraps 3 tbsp + rice salad dressing 4 tbsp — one jar covers both)

Bakery

  • 4 wraps

Frozen

  • Corn on the cob, 4 pieces
  • Sweetcorn, small handful (frozen bag or small tin)

Basics / Packets

  • Pasta, 600g total (chorizo pasta 300g + meatball bake 300g)
  • Rice, 250g
  • Breadcrumbs, 100g
  • Plain flour (small amount — likely already have)
  • 2 tins chopped tomatoes
  • Tomato purée (used across 3 meals — one tube covers it)

Store Cupboard

Check the cupboard first — only buy what you've actually run out of. Most of these should already be in the house from previous weeks.

  • Oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Garlic granules
  • Dried mixed herbs
  • Paprika
  • Smoked paprika
  • Stock cube (beef or chicken)
  • Soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce

New / Occasional Buys This Week

These are long-life items, so if bought for a previous week they'll likely still be in the cupboard. Only add what you're actually out of — they'll be reused again in future weeks.

  • Onion marmalade
  • Mango chutney
  • Dried basil
  • Chilli flakes
  • Cajun seasoning (or make your own from paprika + chilli flakes above)
  • Sriracha
  • Rice vinegar or lime juice
  • Honey (rice salad dressing + gammon glaze — one bottle covers both)
  • Garlic powder (garlic granules can substitute)

Shopping List Optimised: Chicken, cheese, potatoes and red peppers are used across multiple meals to help reduce waste. Every item earns its place — no protein is used more than twice, carbs are balanced (2 pasta dishes, 1 rice dish, chips once, wedges once), and Store Cupboard items are separated from new/occasional buys so you know exactly what's worth checking for before you shop.
