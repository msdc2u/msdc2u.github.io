Week 2 Shopping List

This shopping list has been planned so ingredients work across multiple meals and help reduce waste. All quantities are based on a family of 2 adults and 2 children. Feel free to adjust everything to suit your family — nothing is set in stone.

Fresh

  • 1.5kg potatoes
  • 600g baby potatoes
  • 4 peppers
  • 2 red onions
  • 150g spinach
  • 1 lemon

Meat & Fish

  • 8 boneless chicken thighs
  • 500g pork steaks or strips
  • 12 pork sausages
  • 20 fish fingers
  • 500g beef mince

Dairy

  • 200ml single cream
  • 25g butter
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 150g grated cheddar cheese

Bakery

  • 4 tortilla wraps

Frozen

  • 200g frozen peas

Dry Goods

  • 500g pasta
  • 300g long grain rice

Store Cupboard

  • 400g chopped tomatoes or passata
  • 2 tsp garlic granules
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger (or ½ tsp ground)
  • Cooking oil, salt, black pepper

Shopping List Optimised: Chicken, pork, sausages, fish fingers, potatoes and pasta are used across multiple meals to help reduce waste.
